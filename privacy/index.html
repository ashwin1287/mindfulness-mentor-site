Privacy Policy

Mindful (Mindfulness Mentor) · Last updated 24 November 2026

  • Your history, goals, profile and favourites stay on your phone.
  • Your words leave your phone only when you start a Guided Session, and only after you allow it. They are used to write that one meditation and are not kept.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Who I am

Mindful is made by Ashwin Jiwane, an independent developer. If you have a question about your data, write to ashwinchatgpt177@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your date of birth, country and gender, your answers to the onboarding questions, your goals and their progress, the meditations you have played, your favourites and ratings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. Your country is used on the phone to show the right crisis line.

Your account

To sign in you use your email address and a password, Google, or Apple. Accounts are run by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address, the name you give in the app and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the identifier those services share. When the app asks my server for a meditation, the server checks your sign-in and sees your account ID, which it uses only to limit how many requests one account can make. It does not store it.

What leaves your phone when you make a meditation

Goal steps. The app sends only the kind of meditation (for example, a step on a "calmer at work" path), the time of day, the length and the voice. None of your own words are sent.

Meditations from "Pick what you need right now". The app sends the kind of meditation, the time of day, the length and the voice, plus any feelings you picked and anything you typed about how you feel. My server only matches those against a list of words to choose a theme, such as "stressed". They are not sent to any AI service, not saved and not written to logs.

Both kinds are shared: the same script and recording can be played by anyone who asks for the same kind and theme.

Guided Sessions. When you type how you feel and ask for a meditation written for you, the app first asks your permission. If you allow it:

  • What you typed, and any feelings you picked, are sent to my server and from there to Anthropic (the Claude model), which writes the meditation's script.
  • That script is sent to Google (the Gemini text-to-speech model) to be read aloud.
  • The recording is saved, under a random name that is not linked to you, with Cloudflare R2, so your phone can play it.
  • My server does not keep your words or the script: they are not saved in its database and not written to its logs.

Anthropic and Google process this text under their terms for paid API customers, which say they do not use it to train their models. If you say "Not now", nothing you type is sent, and you can still use card and goal meditations. You can change your answer in Profile.

Before anything is sent, the app checks your message on your phone. If it looks like you may be in crisis, the session stops, nothing is sent, and the app shows you where to get help.

Where my server runs

My server runs on Render. Shared meditation scripts are stored in a Neon database and recordings in Cloudflare R2. These services store data for me and do not use it for their own purposes.

What I don't do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • I never sell or share your data for marketing.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything the app stored on your phone. Your words were never stored on my server, so there is nothing else to delete there. Shared recordings are not linked to you.

Not medical care

Mindful is a mindfulness and wellbeing app. It is not therapy or medical treatment. If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

Children

Mindful is for adults. The app asks for your date of birth and does not let anyone under 18 create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date. A change in what leaves your phone will also be shown in the app before it applies.
